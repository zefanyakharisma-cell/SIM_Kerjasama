SIM Kerja Sama business process

How a partnership document (MoU/MoA) moves through SIM-KS, from proposal to signing to renewal. Drawn in Bizagi Modeler style, with one pool per process and a lane for each actor. The steps and rules come from the database functions the app calls, so the diagrams describe what the system enforces.

PROSES 1

Pengajuan & Persetujuan Kerja Sama

A unit proposes, KUI routes it, approvers decide tier by tier, then the signed document is activated. KUI can also record a document that was signed outside the system.

Drag or scroll sideways to follow the flow. Grey mono text under a step is the document status it sets.

Draft→ Diajukan→ Diproses→ Disposisi – Tier 1·2·3→ Disetujui→ Siap TTD→ Aktif · side states PendingDitolak

Approval tiers

TierPositions

Tier gating

A tier opens once no lower tier in the current round is still unapproved. An empty tier is skipped. recompute_tiers is the only writer of this state.

Four decisions

Setuju opens the next tier. Revisi asks KUI to upload a new draft in place; the SLA keeps running. Pending freezes the document and stops the SLA; reactivating starts a new round from Tier 1. Tolak is final and archives the document as rejected.

SLA per approver

Counted in business days from when the approver's tier opens, excluding weekends, holidays and time in Pending. Yellow at 2 days, red at 4. The daily sweep runs at 05:00 WIB.

PROSES 2

Pembaruan & Evaluasi Kerja Sama

As an active document nears its end date, the owning unit and the partner each evaluate it. KUI opens renewal only when both agree, or after an override with a recorded reason.

Aktif→ Akan Berakhir→ Diarsipkan · reasons superseded_by_renewal expired_without_renewal terminated_early

Reminders

Monthly from 6 months before the end date, then weekly for the last 2 months. Auto Renewed documents have no end date and are skipped.

Two evaluations

The unit fills the faculty evaluation in the app. The lead partner fills theirs at /evaluasi/[token] without logging in. Reopening an evaluation issues a new token and keeps the old answer as history.

Renewal gate

Agreement only marks the document ready. Renewal starts when KUI clicks Mulai Proses Pembaruan; that is when the unit is notified and allowed to upload. buat_proposal_perpanjangan refuses anything else.

Hand-over

The renewal proposal runs through Proses 1. Activating it links the new document to the old one and archives the old one in a single transaction.
